Cartesia transcribes the mic live. When it hears “Hey Arm,” the Pi sends only the words after that phrase to Jev through OpenRouter. Jev chooses the command.

Try “Hey Arm, open the hand”, “close the hand,” “retract,” “extend,” or “dance.” Retract goes to Home. Extend points the arm straight up. Fold is disabled because the wrist camera blocks that position.

Say “Hey Arm” and the command in the same utterance. Keep clear of the arm while it moves. “Pick up the can” is recognized, but it cannot run until the camera can locate the can.
